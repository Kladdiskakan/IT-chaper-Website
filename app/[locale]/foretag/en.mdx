## Do you want your company to be visible to over thousands of IT students? - **Then you have come to the right place!**

The IT Chapter is a chapter with over 1000 students studying in the following programs:

- [Degree Programme in Information and Communication Technology](https://www.kth.se/student/kurser/program/CINTE)
- [Degree Programme in Computer Engineering](https://www.kth.se/student/kurser/program/TIDAB)
- [Bachelor's Programme in Information and Communication Technology](https://www.kth.se/student/kurser/program/TCOMK)
<br /> 
There are many ways to collaborate with the IT-Chapter. ranging from hosting a lunch lecture or a company visit to participating during the reception.
We are happy to tailor a collaboration that aligns with your specific goals and budget. You can also browse our product catalog to explore ready-made packages, special offers, and inspiration on how your company can increase its visibility among our students.
Below is an overview of our most common forms of collaboration.
If you have a unique idea that you think our students would appreciate, don’t hesitate to reach out! We look forward to finding a solution that fits your needs perfectly.

## Events
There are plenty of opportunities to host events. These can range from a lunch lecture on campus to a company visit, or why not a case night in our student chapter hall?

## Reception - Meet our new students

During 3 intensive weeks in August, we welcome approximately 260 new students to the chapter. 
Together with another 50 older students who help organize, the Reception is an excellent opportunity for companies to get exposure. 
Participation for companies can range from hosting an event at their place to being visible on our merch, the nollebook and on the reception page.


## Reality check - Meet our master students
Interested in hungry students who are approaching the job market by leaps and bounds?  
Then Reality check is for you!  

Reality check is a format where we together tailor an event for our master's students. This could mean anything from a study visit where our students get a "reality check" on what a workplace look like.
It could also be holding an event or pub in our chapter hall in Kista.


## Marketing
Reach over 3000 students through a post on our Facebook, Instagram, Discord and Linkedin. A perfect opportunity to spread 
job ads, announce places to write your candidacy or just show that you support the IT Chapter at KTH. In addition to the digital channels, there is also the opportunity 
to be seen on our physical places like the Chapter TV and on posters around the chapter hall.
